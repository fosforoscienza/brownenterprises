About us

Brown Enterprises Srl Semplificata was founded in 2020 by 5 science communicators with a passion for communication, the curiosity to explore new horizons, and the pursuit of innovative solutions to bring the general public closer to culture.

It is made up of a multidisciplinary team with decades of experience in communicating science and culture, in delivering complex and innovative projects for public and private organizations, and in running events and activities for the general public.

Frequently asked questions

What does Brown Enterprises do?
Brown Enterprises Srls is a scientific and cultural communication and outreach company based in Senigallia (AN), Italy. It works in communication (science, art, technology), edutainment (labs, workshops, shows and exhibitions) and training.
What does edutainment mean and how does Brown Enterprises apply it?
Edutainment combines education and entertainment to make learning engaging. Brown Enterprises applies it through labs, workshops, shows and exhibitions designed as real experiences.
What services does Brown Enterprises offer?
Brown Enterprises offers three service areas: communication (of science, art and technology), edutainment and experiences (labs, workshops, shows and exhibitions), and training.
Where is Brown Enterprises located?
Brown Enterprises Srls is based at Via Fattori 10, 60019 Senigallia (AN), Italy. Its VAT number is 02870950421.
How can I contact Brown Enterprises or request a quote?
You can email info@brownenterprises.it or the certified address brownsrls@pec.it, and visit www.brownenterprises.it. Describe your needs in your message to request information or a quote.
What is Seven Wireless?
Seven Wireless is Brown Enterprises' wireless headphones product, currently coming soon.
Who founded Brown Enterprises and who does it work for?
Brown Enterprises was founded in 2020 by five science communicators (Mattia Crivellini, Alessandro Gnucci, Valeria Barattini, Luca Dori and Lorenzo Fiorentino) and works with both public and private organizations on communication, edutainment and training projects.
