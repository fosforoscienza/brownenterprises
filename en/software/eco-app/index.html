Local biodiversity, in your pocket.

Twenty cards to explore nature, a quiz to test yourself, six downloadable teaching units and a guide to the living world on your doorstep. Made for the students and teachers who actually go outside and look.

What it is

ECO App is the application of Ecosistema di Comunità, a project supported by Fondazione Cariverona that connects different communities and territories towards a shared goal: protecting biodiversity and bringing neglected green spaces back to life, with young people at the centre.

The project produces material — cards, guides, workshops, field trips. The app is where that material stops being a scattering of PDFs and becomes something a teenager opens on a phone while standing in a meadow.

What’s inside

Twenty nature cards

From life cycles to biodiversity, from ecosystems to climate. Tap a card and the content opens: read them in order or jump wherever curiosity leads.

The quiz

Ten questions, an explanation after each answer and a score at the end. The explanation matters more than the score: that is where you learn something.

Six teaching units

Biomimicry, ecosystems, the sea in the Anthropocene, soil biology, the herbarium, biodiversity. Each one reads inside the app and downloads as a PDF for the classroom.

Guida Galattica alla Biodiversità

Local species, observation techniques, field activities and thematic deep dives: a journey through the living world of the project’s territories, downloadable in full.

The project and the gallery

The four pillars, the territories involved and photographs of what actually happened in the squares and the parks.

It installs and stays

It opens in a browser and sits on the Home screen. Once opened it works without a network too: in a wood, that is exactly what you need.

The four pillars

They are the project’s goals, and also the thread along which the app’s content is ordered.

1

Raise awareness

Promote environmental sustainability and the care of biodiversity.

2

Give value

Restore marginal green spaces so they become alive and usable again.

3

Belong

Strengthen the sense of community through bottom‑up approaches and shared practice.

4

Take responsibility

Build critical awareness in young people through teaching activities and citizen science.

Who made it

ECO App was born inside Ecosistema di Comunità, with the cultural association NEXT ETS and the students of the technical college of Istituto di Istruzione Superiore Corinaldesi‑Padovano. An app about biodiversity written partly by people still at school: it was the most consistent way to make it.
